# Who he is

- Born in Istanbul in 1985, LA-based media artist
- Founded Refik Anadol Studio with Efsun Erkılıç in 2014
- "Data as pigment, machine intelligence as collaborator"
- Opened DATALAND (an AI art museum) in June 2026

# Three periods

- **2008 to 2015, data painting on architecture**: projection mapping, Infinity Room
- **2016 to 2023, machine hallucinations**: GAN, t-SNE, UMAP, Machine Hallucinations, Unsupervised, Sphere
- **2024 onward, in-house models**: Large Nature Model, Large Architecture Model, DATALAND

# Visual grammar

- Data pigment: values become the color and material of particles
- Data Universe: an archive spread into a 3D nebula
- Latent walk: forms melt into other forms
- Data sculpture: trompe-l'œil volume inside a frame
- Living architecture: projection fitted to a building's form

# Controversy

- Jerry Saltz: "a massive techno lava lamp", "a half-million-dollar screensaver"
- Defense: a historic meeting of immersive installation, NFTs and AI. Average dwell time of 38 minutes
- Shifting debate: from public data to permission-based data, demands to disclose carbon cost

# three.js technique study: Pigment Atlas

- three.js r186, WebGPURenderer + TSL compute, RenderPipeline + bloom
- 100 scenes: 10 themes (Machine Dreams, Atmosphere, Ocean, Forest & Life, Earth, Cosmos, Micro, City, Countryside, Physics & Math) × 10
- 100 non-overlapping concepts managed as a catalog
- Korean/English toggle, fullscreen (Esc to exit), shortcuts, scene index, auto tour
