# Scene Catalog: 10 Themes × 10 = 100

[한국어](../06-scene-catalog.md) | **English**

Principle: each scene covers **one concept**, and no phenomenon or subject appears twice. Scenes that use a similar algorithm (for example, branching) count as separate concepts when their subject and scale differ, and even then their visual treatment is designed to look different. Numbering follows the theme order (theme number × 10 + position).
